Privacy Policy

Calculator

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Calculator does not collect your personal data.

Arithmetic stays on this device. Calculation history is saved in local storage on the phone and is not sent to a server. The app does not require an account or login and does not collect your name, username, email address, contacts, location, photos, or microphone data.

There is no analytics and no advertising in the app.

When you tap Copy, the current number is placed on the system clipboard for that user-requested action. The app does not use the clipboard for analytics, advertising, or transmission to a server.

Key presses may trigger a short vibration. The Android VIBRATE permission is used only to provide key-press feedback.

Data Collection and Sharing

Calculator does not collect or transmit personal or sensitive user data to a server and does not share such data with third parties.

Calculation history remains on the user's device and is not transmitted to us.

Data Retention and Deletion

Calculation history stored locally on the device remains under the user's control. It is not stored on our servers.

Uninstalling the app or clearing the app's data removes locally stored application data according to Android's standard app-data behavior.

Security

Because Calculator does not transmit calculation history or personal information to our servers, we do not maintain a server-side database of user information for this app.

Children's Privacy

Calculator does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.
